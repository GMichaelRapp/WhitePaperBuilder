How to write the body

Charts, tables, timelines, key numbers, and images. Give each one a short name, then place it in the body or an appendix with [[name]] on a line of its own. Figures and tables are numbered in the order they appear.

Each # heading starts Appendix A, B, C. ## headings are numbered A.1, A.2. [[rating-key]] adds the source rating key table.

Cite sources from your OSINT Citation Generator lists. In the generator, save your list to a file; open that file here. Each source gets a short key. Write [@key] or [@key, p. 45] in the text: the first citation of a source prints in full, later ones in short form.

Use *asterisks* for italics. A page number in [@key, p. 4] goes before the final period.

No sources yet. You can still write notes by hand with ^[note text].

The "Cite as" line is built from the title, author, number, version, publisher, date, and link under Paper.
