## Paged.js

Lays the paper out as printed pages: footnotes, running headers, page numbers, and the page numbers in the contents. Version 0.4.3, © 2018 Adam Hyde and the Coko Foundation. MIT License; the full text is in `src/vendor/PAGEDJS-LICENSE.md`. Source: https://gitlab.coko.foundation/pagedjs/pagedjs. The copy in `index.html` is the project's own minified build, `paged.min.js`.

## OSINT Citation Generator core

Formats the footnotes for sources cited from OSINT Citation Generator list files. Version 3.2.1, © 2026 G. Michael Rapp, MIT License. Source: https://github.com/gmichaelrapp/OSINT_Cites. The copy is `src/vendor/osint-core.js`, taken unmodified from that tool's `index.html`.

## IBM Plex

The paper is set in IBM Plex Serif, IBM Plex Sans Condensed, and IBM Plex Mono, with the Cyrillic letters of IBM Plex Sans standing in for Plex Sans Condensed, which has none. © IBM Corp., with Reserved Font Name "Plex". Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, version 1.1; the license text is in `src/vendor/fonts/`. The font files come from the Fontsource packages (`@fontsource/ibm-plex-serif`, `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans-condensed`, `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans`, `@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono`, version 5.3.0). They are embedded in `index.html` unmodified, as Fontsource subsets them: Latin, Latin Extended, and Cyrillic.
